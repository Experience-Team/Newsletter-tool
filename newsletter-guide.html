How to Create a Newsletter

← Back to Editor

Get your newsletter out the door in minutes

This guide walks you through the newsletter editor from start to finish — from planning your content to exporting a ready-to-send HTML file.

Open the Newsletter Editor →
Step 1

Plan your content

Before you open the editor, spend a few minutes gathering everything you need. A little prep goes a long way.

  • Pick a theme or focus. What's the main story this edition? One strong lead beats five scattered updates.
  • Collect your stories. Gather links, copy, images, and contributors you want to include.
  • Check character limits early. Headings should be under 100 characters; body text under 1,500 characters per block.
  • Upload images first. Host all images on Cloudinary before you start — you'll need URLs ready to paste in.
Step 2

Fill in the editor fields

Open the Newsletter Editor and work through the form on the left. The live preview on the right updates as you type.

  1. Header: Set the edition date (e.g. April 2026) and edition number.
  2. Hero image: Paste the Cloudinary URL for your main banner image. Aim for at least 1,200px wide.
  3. Content blocks: Click Add Block to insert sections. Choose from:
    • Standard — heading + body text
    • Image + Text — image alongside a text column
    • Two Column — side-by-side content
    • Feature — full-width highlighted section
  4. Procrastination Station: Add 2–3 interesting links for the closing section.
  5. Footer: The footer is pre-populated — update the date if needed.
Step 3

Add and size your images

Images make the newsletter. Follow these steps to get them in and looking right.

How to upload to Cloudinary:

  1. Go to cloudinary.com and sign in.
  2. Click Upload and drag your image file in.
  3. Once uploaded, click the image and copy the URL from the details panel.
  4. Paste the URL into the relevant image field in the editor.

Image size guidelines:

  • Hero/banner images: 1,200px wide minimum
  • Content images: 600px wide minimum
  • Team/person thumbnails: square crop, face centred
  • File size: under 1 MB per image
  • Avoid SharePoint or internal network links — they won't load for all recipients
Step 4

Preview and export

The right-hand panel in the editor shows a live preview of your newsletter as you work. Use it to sense-check layouts and spot any copy issues.

When you're happy with everything:

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the editor and click Export HTML.
  2. A .html file will download to your computer.
  3. Import this file into your email sending platform (e.g. Campaign Monitor, Mailchimp) or send it to your email team.

Tip: send a test to yourself first and check it in both desktop and mobile email clients before the full send.

Tips & best practices

Write like a human

Warm, conversational, and inclusive. Think colleague over coffee, not corporate memo. Short sentences. Active voice.

Keep paragraphs short

2–3 sentences per paragraph maximum. Walls of text won't get read — break it up and lead with the most interesting detail.

Real photos over stock

Candid team shots and event photos outperform generic stock imagery every time. Authentic beats polished.

De-AI your drafts

If you use AI to help write copy, run it through a de-humanisation pass. Open the Guidelines drawer in the editor for a prompt you can use.

Check character counts

Headings: under 100 characters. Body text: under 1,500 characters per block. The editor will warn you if you go over.

Link smartly

Every link should open in a new tab. Use descriptive link text — avoid "click here". Make links worth clicking.
