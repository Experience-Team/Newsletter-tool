Newsletter Guidelines

Writing Tone

Keep it warm, conversational, and inclusive. Write as though you're talking to a colleague over coffee — not delivering a corporate memo. Avoid jargon unless it's widely understood across the business.

  • Use active voice and short sentences where possible.
  • Lead with the most interesting or relevant detail.
  • Keep paragraphs to 2–3 sentences. Walls of text won't get read.

Image Guidance

Use high-quality images that feel authentic — real team photos, candid shots from events, and project visuals work best. Avoid generic stock photography where possible.

  • Images should be at least 600px wide for full-width sections.
  • Team thumbnails should be square and cropped to show the face clearly.
  • Host all images on Cloudinary or another reliable CDN. Avoid SharePoint or internal links.

Section Usage

Aim for 3–5 content sections per edition. Too many sections means nothing gets the attention it deserves.

  • Standard — Best for announcements, updates, and standalone stories.
  • Image + Text — Good for spotlights, profiles, and visual stories.
  • Two Column — Use for quick-hit updates like dates, stats, or paired items.
  • Feature — Reserve for the single most important story of the edition. The red background draws the eye, so use it sparingly.

Interesting Links

Choose three links that are genuinely useful or inspiring. Mix it up — an industry article, an internal resource, and something fun or creative works well.
